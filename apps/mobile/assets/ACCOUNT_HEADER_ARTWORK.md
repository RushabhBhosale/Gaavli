# Shared account header village artwork

`account-header-village.png` was created with the built-in image_gen tool from
the user-supplied identity-header reference on 2026-10-21. It is bundled locally
and used by AccountHeader on Home and by PageSceneHeader as background on
Settings and Activity. Home renders actual name, role, village and initials;
the latter pages render only their title and subtitle over the scenery.
Decorative artwork is hidden from accessibility. No sample notification count
is added because the current inbox API does not expose unread state.

Final generation prompt:

> Generate only the decorative rural village landscape background matching the
> attached mobile app identity header reference. No UI, no text, no person, no
> letters, no bell, no avatar, no produce crate. Wide panoramic 3:1 illustration
> in the same soft green, pale mint, cream and yellow style: distant pale
> overlapping rolling hills, a small cluster of cream village houses with
> terracotta brown roofs on the right, rounded green trees and slender trees,
> low shrubs, soft yellow-green fields along the bottom. Keep left half and upper
> portion almost empty pale mint #F4F9EC fading seamlessly into the scene.
> Concentrate the houses/trees in the lower right third, just like the supplied
> reference. Clean softly shaded vector-like illustration with gentle gradients,
> crisp enough for small mobile header. Image will be used behind live
> name/role/location and icons. No borders or watermark.
